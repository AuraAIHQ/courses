计算机与人工智能的发展是一部由天才构想、战争需求和数学突破编织而成的宏伟史诗。以下是该领域最关键的几位先驱人物及其核心贡献：

---

## 1. 艾伦·图灵 (Alan Turing) —— 计算机科学与AI之父

图灵为现代计算机提供了逻辑基础，并在纳粹密码破译中发挥了决定性作用。

* **核心贡献：**
* **图灵机 (Turing Machine)：** 提出了一个抽象计算模型，证明了凡是可计算的问题都可以通过算法解决，奠定了通用计算机的基础。
* **图灵测试 (Turing Test)：** 提出了衡量机器是否具有智能的标准，即如果人类无法分辨对话者是人还是机器，则认为机器具有智能。
* **恩尼格玛破译：** 二战期间领导团队破解了德国的Enigma密码。


* **头像与维基：**  | [Alan Turing Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Turing)

## 2. 约翰·冯·诺依曼 (John von Neumann) —— 现代计算机架构之父

他将计算机从“特定用途的机器”变成了“可编程的通用工具”。

* **核心贡献：**
* **冯·诺依曼架构 (Von Neumann Architecture)：** 提出了“存储程序控制”原理，即指令和数据共同存储在内存中。至今几乎所有个人电脑和服务器仍沿用此架构。
* **博弈论：** 在经济学和AI决策逻辑中应用广泛。


* **头像与维基：**  | [John von Neumann Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_von_Neumann)

---

## 3. 克劳德·香农 (Claude Shannon) —— 信息论之父

他让人们意识到，世界上的信息是可以被量化的。

* **核心贡献：**
* **二进制逻辑：** 在其硕士论文中证明了布尔代数可以用于开关电路，这直接导致了数字电路的设计。
* **信息熵 (Entropy)：** 定义了信息的度量单位“比特”（Bit），解决了信息传输的效率和可靠性问题。


* **头像与维基：**  | [Claude Shannon Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Shannon)

---

## 4. 约翰·麦卡锡 (John McCarthy) —— “人工智能”术语的发明者

他是AI作为一门独立学科的奠基人。

* **核心贡献：**
* **命名AI：** 在1956年达特茅斯会议上正式提出了“Artificial Intelligence”这一术语。
* **Lisp语言：** 发明了Lisp编程语言，这在随后的几十年里一直是AI开发的首选语言。
* **垃圾回收 (Garbage Collection)：** 发明了自动内存管理技术。


* **头像与维基：**  | [John McCarthy Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McCarthy_(computer_scientist))

## 5. 杰弗里·辛顿 (Geoffrey Hinton) —— 深度学习教父

他坚持了数十年的神经网络研究，最终引发了当前的AI革命。

* **核心贡献：**
* **反向传播算法 (Backpropagation)：** 使得多层神经网络的训练变得可行。
* **深度学习：** 2012年其团队在ImageNet竞赛中取得突破，开启了AI的深度学习时代。
* **2024年诺贝尔物理学奖：** 因在人工神经网络领域的开创性贡献获奖。


* **头像与维基：**  | [Geoffrey Hinton Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Hinton)

---

## 6. 雅恩·乐昆 (Yann LeCun) —— 卷积神经网络之父

他解决了计算机“看懂”世界的关键问题。

* **核心贡献：**
* **卷积神经网络 (CNN)：** 模仿人类视觉皮层结构，发明了LeNet-5，是现代计算机视觉、人脸识别和自动驾驶的底层核心。
* **开源AI：** 作为Meta首席AI科学家，推动了PyTorch等重要工具的开源。


* **头像与维基：**  | [Yann LeCun Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yann_LeCun)

Here is the English version of the key figures in the history of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 1. Alan Turing — The Father of Theoretical Computer Science and AI

Turing provided the logical framework for the modern computer and played a pivotal role in breaking Nazi codes during WWII.

* **Core Contributions:**
* **The Turing Machine:** An abstract mathematical model that defined the limits of what can be computed, forming the foundation of all modern computers.
* **The Turing Test:** Proposed a criterion for machine intelligence: if a human cannot distinguish between a machine and a human in conversation, the machine is said to "think."
* **Enigma Decryption:** Led the team at Bletchley Park to crack the German Enigma code, shortening the war by years.


* **Wiki:** [Alan Turing Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Turing)

---

## 2. John von Neumann — The Father of Modern Computer Architecture

He transformed computers from "fixed-purpose calculators" into "programmable general-purpose tools."

* **Core Contributions:**
* **Von Neumann Architecture:** Proposed the "Stored-Program" principle, where instructions and data are stored in the same memory. This remains the blueprint for nearly every PC and server today.
* **Game Theory:** Developed the mathematical framework for strategic decision-making, which is fundamental to modern AI agents and economics.


* **Wiki:** [John von Neumann Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_von_Neumann)

---

## 3. Claude Shannon — The Father of Information Theory

He realized that information itself is a physical quantity that can be measured and digitized.

* **Core Contributions:**
* **Digital Circuit Design:** Proved in his master’s thesis that Boolean algebra could be used to design switching circuits, essentially creating the digital logic used in all CPUs.
* **Information Entropy:** Defined the "Bit" as the fundamental unit of information, solving the problem of how to transmit data efficiently and reliably.


* **Wiki:** [Claude Shannon Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Shannon)

---

## 4. John McCarthy — The Man who Coined "Artificial Intelligence"

He was the founding father of AI as a formal academic discipline.

* **Core Contributions:**
* **Naming AI:** Formally introduced the term "Artificial Intelligence" at the 1956 Dartmouth Workshop.
* **Lisp Language:** Created the Lisp programming language, which became the standard for AI research for decades.
* **Garbage Collection:** Invented automatic memory management, a feature used in modern languages like Java and Python.


* **Wiki:** [John McCarthy Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McCarthy_(computer_scientist))

---

## 5. Geoffrey Hinton — The Godfather of Deep Learning

His decades of persistence in neural network research eventually triggered the current AI revolution.

* **Core Contributions:**
* **Backpropagation:** Popularized the algorithm that allows multi-layer neural networks to "learn" from errors.
* **Deep Learning:** His team’s breakthrough in the 2012 ImageNet competition proved that deep neural networks were superior to traditional AI methods.
* **2024 Nobel Prize in Physics:** Awarded for foundational discoveries and inventions that enable machine learning with artificial neural networks.


* **Wiki:** [Geoffrey Hinton Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Hinton)

---

## 6. Yann LeCun — The Father of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)

He solved the problem of how computers "see" and recognize patterns in images.

* **Core Contributions:**
* **CNNs:** Inspired by the human visual cortex, he developed LeNet-5, the architectural ancestor of all modern facial recognition, medical imaging, and autonomous driving systems.
* **Open-Source Advocacy:** As Chief AI Scientist at Meta, he has championed the release of open-source AI tools like PyTorch.


* **Wiki:** [Yann LeCun Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yann_LeCun)
